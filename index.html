Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Who is this academy for?

It is designed for serious growers, landowners, agripreneurs, and future-facing stewards looking to build regenerative systems with depth and strategic intent.

Do I need farming experience?

Not necessarily. The academy is structured to help both new and experienced practitioners build clarity, skill, and confidence in regenerative farm design.

Is this just theory?

No. Every module includes actionable frameworks, templates, implementation prompts, and a final capstone to create a realistic farm plan.

Can I scale this into a business?

Yes. The curriculum includes business strategy, market positioning, financial modeling, and scaling frameworks to support long-term profitability.
